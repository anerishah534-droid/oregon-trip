⚠ Book / know before you go

🎟️
Multnomah Falls needs a timed permit ($2) for Wed Aug 5 if you park at the main I-84 Exit 31 lot between 9am–6pm. The 14-day release already happened (Jul 22) — check recreation.gov now for leftover slots, or grab one in the second release Mon Aug 3 at 7:00am PT. No-permit plan B: arrive after 6pm (sunset ≈ 8:30pm, gorgeous light) — that actually fits your drive perfectly.
🚧
Cape Lookout State Park closes Aug 3, 2026 — the exact day you drive the Three Capes loop — for a construction project. Plan around it: Cape Meares + Cape Kiwanda are open, and Cascade Head is your hike instead.
🚧
Hug Point is closed indefinitely (storm damage) — Arcadia Beach, 1 mile south of Cannon Beach, is the stand-in. Ecola State Park is open again, but the Clatsop Loop's west section is closed.
💳
Fees to expect: Oregon State Parks day-use went up in 2026 — $10–12/day, but one daily permit is valid at all charging state parks that day, so Sunday's single pass covers Ecola + Oswald West (+ Fort Stevens). Yaquina Head is $7/vehicle. Tumalo Falls is $5 or a NW Forest Pass. Blue Pool and Sahalie/Koosah are free.
🔥
August is fire season. No closures on your routes as of late July, but re-check tripcheck.com before the Newport→Bend and Bend→Portland drives.

2≈12:45–1:40🎪Astoria Sunday Market — lunch

🚗 2 hrs from PDXVeg-friendlyMust-do

You land in Astoria on market day: 100+ vendors, live music, and food stalls filling 12th Street downtown, 10am–3pm. Graze your lunch stall-to-stall and soak up the town. (Blue Scorcher, the great vegetarian bakery, is 2 blocks away if you want a proper pastry course.)

12th St, downtownFree entry

3≈1:40–2:35🚶Riverwalk stroll → Coffee Girl at Pier 39

Riverfront walkCoffee

From the market, wander the flat riverfront path east under the big bridge views (~1 mi) to Pier 39 — the old Bumble Bee tuna cannery on pilings over the Columbia. Coffee Girl inside is a beloved local coffeehouse where you sip espresso literally over the river (weekends 8am–3pm). Bonus: the East Mooring Basin docks next door are usually piled with barking sea lions. Walk back through town, or hop the 1913 Riverfront Trolley ($2 all-day, Sun 11–5).

Flat, ~2 mi round tripFree (trolley $2)

🚗 8:00 · Lincoln City → Newport · 25 mi of great pullouts — pace yourself, the mountains are waiting

1–2≈8:15–9:00🐋Boiler Bay + Depoe Bay seawall

🚗 15 min · seawall 🚶Scenic

Boiler Bay is a 10-min clifftop pullout; then watch for spouts from the Depoe Bay seawall — resident gray whales feed close to shore all summer (staff see 5–15 a day). World's smallest harbor, too.

Free30–50 min combined
The Whale Watching Center building is closed Tuesdays — the seawall itself is the show anyway.

3–4≈9:10–9:50🌀Otter Crest Loop → Devils Punchbowl

🚗 scenic loop driveScenic

Take the one-way southbound old highway loop past Cape Foulweather (500 ft above the surf), ending near Devils Punchbowl — a collapsed sea cave that churns at high tide.

Free40–60 min

5≈10:00–11:30🗼Yaquina Head + tidepools

🚗 15 min → 🚶 to poolsScenicMust-do

Oregon's tallest lighthouse (1873) on a basalt headland — and your tidepool moment: low tide hits ≈11am, prime time for the Cobble Beach pools (sea stars, anemones, urchins) with harbor seals on the rocks offshore. Tower stairs are closed for restoration, but pools + grounds are the draw. Sturdy shoes — the cobbles are slick.

$7/vehicleVerify tide time day-of

6≈11:40–12:15🥪Nye Beach lunch · Newport

🚗 10 minVeg-friendlyCoffee

NW Coast St

9≈4:35–6:35🔵Tamolitch Blue Pool

🚗 12 min → 🚶 4-mi hikeHike · 4 mi RTThe blue hole!

The McKenzie River emerges from underground lava into an unreal glowing turquoise pool. ~4 mi round trip, gentle (~285 ft), from the Trail Bridge Reservoir trailhead (FR 730). Tuesday afternoon is a decent crowd window.

Free · no permit~2 hrsVault toilet, no water
Look, don't swim: the water is 37°F year-round and cliff-jumping here has killed people. It's a viewpoint-from-the-rim kind of place.

8≈6:15–7:30🎟Multnomah Falls

🚗 15 min back east → 🚶 paved pathThe double waterfallHeadliner

Oregon's 620-ft two-tier icon. Walk up to Benson Bridge (paved, 5 min) between the tiers; the switchback trail to the top is 2.4 mi round trip / 600 ft if you want to earn dinner.

After 6pm: no permit neededSunset ≈ 8:30pm
These timings land you at the I-84 lot just after 6pm — no permit needed, golden-hour light, thinner crowds. Backup: a 3–5pm timed slot ($2, recreation.gov, second release Aug 3 at 7am PT) if you'd rather go earlier. Lodge restrooms are under renovation — portable toilets on site.
